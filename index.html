Beyond the Shadows

by Brent Weeks

Synopsis

In a world where shadows hold power, destinies collide. Beyond the Shadows follows a gripping tale of sacrifice, darkness, and redemption as heroes rise to confront ancient forces.

“The shadows whispered, but he walked forward anyway.”

About the Author

Brent Weeks is a bestselling fantasy author known for immersive worlds and unforgettable characters.

Book Details

Kylar Stern, originally Azoth, is the protagonist of the Night Angel Trilogy. Trained as an assassin by the legendary Durzo Blint, he struggles with his dark past while trying to protect those he loves. Kylar is a complex hero who faces impossible choices, balancing morality, loyalty, and the immense power he wields through the magical “Talent.”

The story takes place in a gritty, dark fantasy world dominated by political intrigue, crime, and war. The central kingdom of Cenaria faces threats from powerful neighboring nations and shadowy guilds. Magic exists but is rare and dangerous, often tied to assassins and those with the “Talent.” The world is atmospheric, harsh, and morally gray, making every decision life-altering.

Magic in the Night Angel Trilogy is rare and feared, and is tied closely to assassins, who use it to enhance their skills. The “Talent” allows Kylar and others to perform incredible feats, but it comes with personal cost and moral challenges. The assassin guild, secretive and powerful, influences politics and events behind the scenes, making every shadow a place of danger.
